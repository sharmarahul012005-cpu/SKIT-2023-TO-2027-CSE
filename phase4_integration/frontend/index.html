How This Works

This project estimates millet price and demand using a simple formula based on factors that commonly affect agricultural markets, such as:

  • Season / month of the year
  • Rainfall
  • Price of a substitute crop (wheat)
  • Recent price and demand trend

It is meant as a simple, explainable demo of the idea behind price/demand prediction for a millet value-chain project — it is not connected to a real dataset or a trained ML model.
